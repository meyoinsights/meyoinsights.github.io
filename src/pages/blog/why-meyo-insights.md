## The pursuit of better investment judgment

Buying shares is easy. Understanding what you own is much harder.

A share price tells us what someone is willing to pay today. It does not tell us whether a company earns enough, reinvests wisely, or can withstand changes in its industry.

**Meyo Insights is a public journal of investment research and learning.**

We study companies, industries, markets, and the ideas that help us understand them. Research may begin with an annual report, a regulatory change, an unusual business, or a market development worth investigating.

We read financial statements, examine business economics, look beyond headline profits, and test what the evidence supports. We also draw on industry reports, public data, and the work of other investors—with attribution and a willingness to challenge their conclusions as well as our own.

Kenya and East Africa are our starting point, not our limit.

Some studies will lead to a valuation. Others will explain how an industry works or why two sets of financial figures disagree. Sometimes the useful conclusion is that **we do not know enough yet**.

We intend to show the sources behind our work, distinguish facts from assumptions, explain calculations where they matter, and revisit our views when better evidence appears.

The aim is not to predict every price movement or publish confident stock tips. It is to understand businesses more deeply and, over time, make better investment decisions.

This is a journal of that pursuit.
