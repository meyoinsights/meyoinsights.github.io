Meyo Insights is a public record of learning to invest better. We study businesses, industries, markets and investment ideas, then follow the evidence wherever it leads.

Our process guides the research; it is not a claim that every investigation completes every step.

## 1. Find something worth understanding

We explore companies, industries, market changes and investing ideas. A promising business or unusual development is enough to start.

## 2. Build a reliable evidence base

We gather annual reports, filings, official data and independent research. We check sources, dates and limitations, and flag what remains unverified.

## 3. Understand the business and its industry

We examine customers, costs, competition, regulation and how the business earns money. We also consider management and capital allocation.

## 4. Read the accounts carefully

We study profits, cash flow, debt and financial-statement notes. Where available, we compare five years of consistent figures, accounting for restatements and reporting differences.

## 5. Separate business quality, value and price

A good company is not always a good investment. We estimate value using supported assumptions, test different outcomes and compare value with market price.

## 6. Account for risk and cycles

We examine debt, competition, regulation and economic cycles. We test the opposing case and identify evidence that could change our view.

## 7. Explain what we found

We write clearly, show important calculations and cite sources. We separate facts from judgment and state uncertainties rather than hide them.

## 8. Review, publish and revisit

We check our research and approve public writing deliberately. Software and AI assist, but do not decide. New evidence or errors prompt corrections and fresh analysis.

**The pursuit of better investment judgment is a continuing practice, not a destination.**
